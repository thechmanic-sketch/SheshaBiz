First install

What to expect the first time.

SheshaBiz isn't on the Play Store or signed with a paid Windows certificate yet, so both platforms show a routine warning before letting you install. Here's exactly what to click.

AndroidTap the downloaded file, then "Install anyway" if you see "Unknown app" — this just means it's not from the Play Store, not that anything's wrong.
WindowsClick "More info" on the SmartScreen popup, then "Run anyway". This appears for any new app until it's been downloaded enough times to build reputation with Microsoft.
Either waySign up with your email and a password once — that same login works across Android, Windows, and the web app, with your data synced between all three.

Not ready to install? Try it in the browser.

The web app runs everything SheshaBiz does — no download needed, works on any device with a browser.
